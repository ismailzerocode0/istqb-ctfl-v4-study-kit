# ISTQB CTFL v4.0 Study Kit

An unofficial, AI-assisted 9-day study plan and cheat sheet for the ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) v4.0 exam.

> **Disclaimer:** This is an independent study resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by ISTQB. It was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by me, but always verify definitions against the official CTFL v4.0 syllabus.

## What's inside

| [`docs/02-cheat-sheet.pdf`](docs/02-cheat-sheet.pdf) | Final review sheet covering all six chapters |

## Exam facts used in this kit

- 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes (+15 minutes if English is not your first language)
- Pass mark: 65% (26 of 40)
- Question distribution per ISTQB Exam Structure Tables:

| Ch. | Topic | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fundamentals of Testing | 8 |
| 2 | Testing Throughout the SDLC | 6 |
| 3 | Static Testing | 4 |
| 4 | Test Analysis and Design | 11 |
| 5 | Managing the Test Activities | 9 |
| 6 | Test Tools | 2 |

## A note on AI-generated study material

My first AI draft listed a wrong question distribution and labeled it "official". I caught it by checking against the ISTQB exam structure document, and the plan was rebuilt around the correct numbers. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

If AI helps you study, verify it the way you would verify any build.

## How to use it

1. Read the plan once, then follow one day at a time.
2. Close your notes before drilling. Recall beats re-reading.
3. Keep an error notebook and re-solve your own mistakes.
4. Use official ISTQB sample exams as your mock exams.
